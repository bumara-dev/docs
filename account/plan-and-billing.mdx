Your plan determines which regulators you can connect, how many obligations and service requests
you carry, how many people you can invite, and how much storage you have.

## The plans

<Note>
  Prices are shown as a guide. The current price and what your plan includes are always confirmed
  in **Settings → Plan and billing** before you commit to a change.
</Note>

| | Start | Plus | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly price** | K1,500 | K3,500 | K7,500 | Custom |
| **Obligations** | 3 | 4 | 7 | Unlimited |
| **Service requests** | 2 per month | 3 per month | 5 per month | Unlimited |
| **Users** | 1 | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 500 MB | 1 GB | 5 GB | Custom |
| **Regulators** | ZRA, NAPSA, PACRA | + NHIMA, ZPPA | + WCF, local councils | All |

## Choosing a plan

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start">
    A sole trader or a very small business with straightforward obligations and one person doing
    everything.

    Three obligations covers a typical small company: PAYE, one other ZRA tax type, and a PACRA
    annual return. Two service requests a month is enough for occasional one-off work.

    Outgrow it when you need a second user, or NHIMA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Plus">
    A small business with employees. Adds NHIMA, which you need the moment you employ anyone, and
    a second user so the bookkeeper is not sharing the owner's login.

    Payroll becomes available as an add-on at this tier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro">
    A growing business with a real team. Five users lets you separate payroll from finance, seven
    obligations covers a fuller compliance load, and you gain WCF and local council coverage.

    Right for anyone with more than a handful of staff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enterprise">
    Larger businesses, groups, and consultants managing many client organisations. Everything
    unlimited, with terms agreed individually.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What counts against your limits

| Limit | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Obligations** | Active recurring requirements. A paused obligation does not count |
| **Service requests** | Requests created in the current month. Resets monthly |
| **Users** | Active members. Pending invitations count |
| **Storage** | Total size of everything in your document vault |
| **Regulators** | Which bodies you can connect, not how many connections |

## When you reach a limit

Bumara tells you before you are blocked.

| Limit | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Service requests** | You cannot create more this month. Upgrade, or wait for the reset |
| **Users** | You cannot invite anyone else. Upgrade, or remove someone |
| **Storage** | You cannot upload. Add the storage add-on, or remove unneeded files |
| **Obligations** | You cannot activate another. Upgrade, or pause one you no longer need |
| **Regulators** | The regulator is not offered. Upgrade to the tier that includes it |

If a limit is blocking compliance work, you receive a message about it. Do not simply wait — a
blocked filing still has its deadline. See
[Messages and timeline](/compliance/messages-and-timeline).

## Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings, then Plan and billing">
    Only Owners can change the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your new plan">
    The difference in what you get is shown before you commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The new limits apply immediately. Billing is adjusted from the change date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use what you have gained">
    Connect the additional regulators, invite the additional users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading

<Warning>
  Before downgrading, check you are within the lower plan's limits. If you have five users and
  downgrade to a two-user plan, you must remove three people first. The same applies to
  obligations, storage and connected regulators.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring yourself within the lower limits first">
    Remove users, pause obligations, reduce storage, disconnect regulators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the plan">
    Settings → Plan and billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check nothing has been lost">
    Particularly regulator connections. Disconnecting a regulator in Bumara does not deregister
    you with that regulator — your obligations to them continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding payroll

Payroll is charged per employee, on Plus and Pro.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings, then Plan and billing">
    Choose **Add payroll**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your employee count">
    This is what you are billed on. It updates as your headcount changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payroll up">
    See [Setting up payroll](/payroll/setting-up).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Cycle** | Monthly |
| **Payment** | Card or another method available to your account |
| **Invoices** | Issued each cycle and available in Settings |
| **Currency** | ZMW |

Your subscription charges are separate from regulator fees. Regulator and handling fees for
filings and services are billed per item — see [Paying fees](/compliance/paying-fees).

## If a payment fails

<Steps>
  <Step title="You are notified immediately">
    By email and in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your payment method">
    Settings → Plan and billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry the payment">
    From the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act promptly">
    A prolonged failed payment can restrict your account. Compliance deadlines do not pause while
    your billing is unresolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Overages

Some items are included up to a limit and charged beyond it. Anything charged as an overage is
labelled as such before you commit to it — you are never billed for something you were not shown
the price of.
