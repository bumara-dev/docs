## Bumara terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Obligation** | A recurring thing you must file, such as a monthly PAYE return. Created when you connect a regulator |
| **Filing** | One period of an obligation — "PAYE for March 2026" — with its own due date |
| **Service request** | A one-off job with a regulator, such as registering a company or changing a director |
| **Task** | One step inside a filing or service request. Filings are not ready until required tasks are done |
| **Blocker** | Something stopping a filing from being submitted — a missing task, document or payment |
| **Readiness** | Whether a filing has cleared all three gates and can be submitted |
| **Submission request** | Asking the Bumara team to lodge a filing with the regulator on your behalf |
| **Gate snapshot** | A permanent record of exactly what was complete at the moment you requested submission |
| **Ticket** | A structured message from the Bumara team about a specific filing or request |
| **Timeline** | The unalterable history of everything that has happened to a record |
| **Document vault** | Where all your documents are held, attached to the records they belong to |
| **Health check** | A review of your standing at every regulator. Also called a liability assessment |
| **Finding** | One issue discovered during a health check |
| **Recommendation** | What Bumara suggests you do about a finding |
| **Health score** | A 0–100 rating of your compliance position |
| **Organisation** | Your business in Bumara. One login can belong to several |
| **Authorised representative** | The named person allowed to act for your business with a regulator |

## Regulators

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **PACRA** | Patents and Companies Registration Agency — company and business name registration |
| **ZRA** | Zambia Revenue Authority — taxes |
| **NAPSA** | National Pension Scheme Authority — employee pensions |
| **NHIMA** | National Health Insurance Management Authority — health insurance |
| **WCF** | Workers' Compensation Fund — workplace injury levy |
| **ZPPA** | Zambia Public Procurement Authority |
| **Local council** | Trading licences and business levies |

## Tax terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **TPIN** | Taxpayer Identification Number — ten characters, required for almost everything |
| **PAYE** | Pay As You Earn — tax deducted from employees' pay and remitted to ZRA |
| **VAT** | Value Added Tax — charged on sales if you are registered for it |
| **Turnover tax** | A simplified regime: 5% of gross turnover, for businesses under K5,000,000 annually |
| **Withholding tax** | Tax you deduct when paying for certain services, and remit on the supplier's behalf |
| **Gross emoluments** | An employee's total earnings before deductions |
| **Chargeable emoluments** | The taxable portion of an employee's earnings |
| **Nil return** | A return filed for a period with no activity. Still required |
| **Tax clearance certificate** | ZRA confirmation that your tax affairs are in order. Required for tenders |
| **Smart Invoice** | ZRA's system requiring sales invoices to be transmitted electronically |
| **VSDC** | The device registered with ZRA for Smart Invoice transmission |

## Company terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Entity type** | What kind of business you are — company, business name, partnership, trust or NGO |
| **Limited by shares** | A company where liability is limited to the value of shares held |
| **Limited by guarantee** | A company where members guarantee a fixed amount. Used by non-profits |
| **Business name** | A registered trading name. Not a separate legal entity — liability is personal |
| **Annual return** | A yearly PACRA filing confirming your registered particulars |
| **Financial year end** | The date your accounting year closes. Drives annual return due dates |
| **Name clearance** | PACRA approval of a proposed business name, before registration |
| **Strike-off** | Removal from the register for persistent non-compliance. The company ceases to exist |
| **Restoration** | Getting a struck-off company back on the register |
| **Registered office** | The official address where correspondence is sent |

## Invoicing terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Quote** | An estimate sent before invoicing. Converts to an invoice when accepted |
| **Credit note** | A document reducing what a customer owes you |
| **Debit note** | A document reducing what you owe a vendor |
| **Delivery note** | Documents goods dispatched, without charging |
| **Purchase order** | Your formal order to a vendor |
| **Aging** | Grouping what is owed by how overdue it is |
| **Allocation** | Linking a payment to the specific invoices it settles |
| **Void** | Cancelling a document. It stays visible, marked void |
| **Statement** | Every transaction with one customer or vendor, with the balance |

## Payroll terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pay run** | One month's payroll processing |
| **Monthly inputs** | The figures that vary each month — absence, overtime, advances |
| **Cutoff day** | When monthly inputs must be submitted |
| **Payment day** | When staff are paid |
| **Allowance** | An amount added to pay — housing, transport, meal |
| **Deduction** | An amount taken from pay |
| **Basic salary** | Contractual pay before allowances. NHIMA is calculated on this |
| **Gross pay** | Basic plus allowances plus overtime. NAPSA is calculated on this |
| **Net pay** | What the employee actually receives |
| **Deduction cap** | The maximum share of gross that can be deducted, commonly 50% |
| **Payslip** | The employee's record of what they earned and what was deducted |

## Inventory terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Item** | Anything you hold in stock |
| **Location** | Anywhere stock is held — a shop, warehouse or vehicle |
| **Movement** | A permanent record of a stock change |
| **Balance** | The current quantity of an item at a location |
| **Adjustment** | An operation increasing or decreasing stock, with a reason |
| **Transfer** | Moving stock between your own locations |
| **In transit** | Shipped but not yet received |
| **Stock count** | Comparing physical stock against records |
| **Variance** | The difference between counted and system quantities |
| **Posting** | Making a draft operation real. Irreversible except by voiding |
| **Reorder level** | The quantity at which you want to be warned |
| **Lay-by** | Goods held for a customer paying in instalments |
| **Cash-out** | Money taken out of the till |
| **SKU** | Your unique code for an item |

## Status words

| Word | Generally means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being prepared. Nothing has taken effect |
| **Pending** | Waiting for something |
| **In progress** | Work has begun |
| **Ready** | Everything required is complete |
| **Submitted** | Sent to the regulator |
| **Accepted** | Confirmed by the regulator |
| **Needs correction** | Returned with something to fix |
| **Posted** | Made permanent |
| **Void** | Reversed, but still visible |
| **Locked** | Permanently sealed |
