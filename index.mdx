  **About the figures in these guides.** Tax rates, contribution rates, thresholds, deadlines and regulator fees change — sometimes annually. Everything here describes how Bumara works and what the rules are as we apply them, but it is guidance, not tax or legal advice. The regulator's current published position always governs, and the figures on your actual filing are the ones that count. For how the rules apply to your particular business, speak to a qualified accountant or tax adviser.
