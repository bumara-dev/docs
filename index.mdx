Bumara is a multi-tenant ERP and compliance platform. It keeps a business current with
its regulators (PACRA, ZRA, NAPSA, NHIMA) while running the day to day operations that
generate the filings: payroll, invoicing, inventory, and documents.

These docs cover the whole system: how to get it running locally, how it is put
together, and how each feature behaves.
