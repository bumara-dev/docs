## How to Use These Personas

When making any product decision, ask:

- "Would this person understand it on first use on mobile?"
- "Does this person’s day get faster or safer (compliance-wise) because of this?"
- "Is the price within their willingness to pay?"

## Primary Persona: Business Owner

### Quick Summary

A hands-on owner who wears multiple hats (sales, ops, finance lite). Lives in WhatsApp and spreadsheets, constantly chasing updates from admin and accounts. Success = fewer time-consuming processes, faster turnarounds, clean payments, and no regulator surprises—so he can focus on customer satisfaction and growth.

### Demographics

- **Name:** Chandamali
- **Age:** 25
- **Location:** Lusaka, Zambia
- **Company Type:** Tech & outsourcing services (e.g., domestic care/field services)
- **Company Size:** 5
- **Role:** CEO/COO (plays multiple roles)
- **Years of Experience:** 5
- **Education:** Diploma
- **Tech Savviness:** Basic

### Professional Life

**Reports To:** The Board of Directors

**Manages:** 5 staff (admin/secretary, customer support, accountant, developer, trainer) + external service providers

**Daily Responsibilities:**

- Engage prospects and partners (e.g., caregivers, maids); handle rush orders
- Oversee payroll for employees and payouts for service partners; ensure PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA compliance
- Coordinate with Accounts for invoice/receipt status and cash collection
- Track regulator-related operational docs (e.g., vehicle fitness/insurance for field visits)
- Handle customer escalations and refunds/credits

**A Day in Their Life:**
Starts 07:00 by scanning WhatsApp/customer emails for new bookings and complaints. Jumps into a spreadsheet to check bookings vs. staff availability, then calls the accountant to confirm yesterday’s receipts. Before lunch, meets a prospect; approves two urgent bookings. Afternoon is firefighting: rescheduling a no-show, asking support to update the client, and phoning the accountant for PAYE status. Leaves office after 20:30, still unsure if all invoices were sent and whether NHIMA returns are queued.

### Technical Profile

**Current Tools:**

- Google sheets or Excel
- WhatsApp
- Email

**Devices:**

- Primary: Windows laptop
- Secondary: Android phone (heavy day time use)

**Computer Skills:** [Rate 1-10: 5/10]

**Internet Access:**

- At work: Reliable(occasionally slow)
- At home: Spotty
- Mobile data: Yes

**Software Experience:**

- Comfortable with: Sheets, WhatsApp, basic accounting UI, ChatGPT for drafting
- Struggles with: Multi-step setups, report builders, file naming/versioning

### Goals & Motivations

**Professional Goals:**

1. Keep utilization high without missing bookings
2. Get paid faster (reduce follow-ups and write-offs)
3. Stay fully compliant (no penalties, predictable filings)

**Personal Motivations:**

- Build a reputation for reliability and professionalism
- Grow to 50+ headcount in 18–24 months
- Spend evenings with family instead of chasing updates and operational processes

**Success Looks Like:**
A week where bookings, payouts, and filings run “on rails”: one screen shows cash due, tasks due, and what’s at risk; invoices go out same day; compliance reminders are handled on time.

### Pain Points & Frustrations

**Problems with Current System:**

1. **Problem 1 - No single source of truth for bookings, payouts, and invoices**
   - Frequency: Daily
   - Impact: Missed revenue; angry clients/maids; rework
   - Current workaround: WhatsApp + spreadsheets + phone calls
   - Quote: “20 people call me to answer one question: are we paid?”

2. **Problem 2 - Compliance tasks are invisible until they’re late**
   - Frequency: Monthly/quarterly
   - Impact: Penalties and stress
   - Current workaround: Calendar reminders no one maintains
   - Quote: “Regulators remind me with a penalty.”

3. **Problem 3 - Manual payroll/payouts cause errors**
   - Frequency: Monthly
   - Impact: Over/under payments; disputes with partners
   - Current workaround: Ad-hoc spreadsheets and mobile money screenshots
   - Quote: “I don’t want to audit Momo mobile money screenshots at midnight.”

**Biggest Frustration:**
Chasing basic status (paid/not paid, due/not due) across apps and people.

**Emotional State:**

- When things go wrong: Anxious → Cant make clear business decisions
- About their tools: “it works… until it doesn’t”
- About their job: Loves growth & customers; hates admin chaos

### Needs & Requirements

**Must Have (Deal Breakers):**

- [ ] One dashboard for cash (invoices, receipts), obligations (PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA), and today’s tasks
- [ ] Easy invoicing + receipt capture + customer reminders
- [ ] Compliance calendar with auto-reminders and evidence storage

**Want to Have (Important):**

- [ ] Simple payouts checklist (who to pay, when, and why)
- [ ] Mobile approvals (invoice send, payout ok, submission ok)
- [ ] Basic service catalog and pricing

**Nice to Have (Bonus):**

- [ ] WhatsApp-style shareable invoice links
- [ ] KPI tiles (DSO, on-time filing rate, refunds rate)

**Won't Use (Avoid These):**

- ❌ Long setup before first value
- ❌ Desktop-only experiences
- ❌ Black-box automations with no override

### How They'll Use Bumara

**Frequency:** 3–8 sessions/day

**Peak Usage Times:**

- 07:00–09:00 (planning/approvals)
- 16:00–19:00 (collections/status checks)

**Typical Session:**

1. Open dashboard (cash, obligations due, risk alerts)
2. Approve/send invoices or reminders; check receipts
3. Review upcoming filings; attach evidence if needed
4. 3–6 minutes per session

**Device Preference:**

- Desktop: 40%
- Mobile: 60%
- Tablet: 0%

**Top 5 Features They'll Use:**

1. Compliance dashboard, reminders & regulatory fillings
2. Invoices/receipts + due reminders (smart invoicing ZRA)
3. Salaries Payouts checklist
4. Customer directory & history
5. Evidence vault storage for filings

### Quote from Persona

> “Put money due and filings due, how the business is doing financially on the same screen and let me approve and delegate things on my phone—no training needed.”

### Success Criteria

**Chandamali is successful with Bumara when:**

- ✅ On-time statutory submissions ≥98% by month 3
- ✅ 90% of invoices sent same-day
- ✅ All statutory obligations (NAPSA, ZRA, NHIMA) handled with ease on the same platform
- ✅ Zero penalties in a rolling quarter

**Time Saved:** 5–8 hours/week  
**Cost Saved:** $100–$500/month (penalties + write-offs)
**Stress Reduced:** “less evening admin” by month 2

## Secondary Persona: Bookkeeper / Assistant Accountant

### Quick Summary 2

Detail-oriented finance doer who keeps daily books clean: posts invoices/receipts, reconciles payments, prepares PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA packs, and nudges ops for missing evidence. Success = accurate journals, zero late filings, and fewer end-of-month surprises.

### Demographics 2

- **Name:** Mutinta
- **Age:** 33
- **Location:** Lusaka, Zambia
- **Company Type:** Retail/Distribution
- **Company Size:** 40
- **Role:** Assistant accountant
- **Years of Experience:** 3
- **Education:** Degree in Accounting/Finance; ZICA Tech/Adv Cert in progress
- **Tech Savviness:** Intermediate

### Professional Life 2

**Reports To:** Finance Manager (or Business Owner if no FM)

**Manages:** — (individual contributor; may mentor an intern)

**Daily Responsibilities:**

- Capture sales invoices and issue receipts
- Reconcile customer payments (bank, MoMo); follow up on under/over payments
- Maintain supplier bills and simple expense claims
- Prepare monthly PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA data exports and evidence bundle
- File documents (invoices, GRNs, acknowledgements) for audit readiness

**A Day in their Life:**

Starts 08:00 checking bank/MoMo statements. Posts previous day invoices and receipts, flags short pays. Late morning compiles PAYE/NAPSA counts and uploads evidence. Afternoon reconciles supplier bills and updates the aging report. Closes with a quick dashboard scan: overdue AR, obligations due this week, and missing documents.

### Technical Profile 2

**Current Tools:**

- Sage/QuickBooks (or basic), Excel/Google Sheets, email, shared drive/WhatsApp for docs

**Devices:**

- Windows laptop (primary), Android phone (secondary for approvals/notifications)

**Computer Skills:** Rate 1-10: 7/10 (comfortable with CSVs, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP; basic pivot tables)

**Internet Access:**

- Reliable at office; mobile data backup

**Software Experience:**

- Comfortable with: Posting entries, imports/exports, PDF receipts, basic reports
- Struggles with: Complex report builders, multi-step portal workflows (ZRA, NAPSA), chasing evidence across

### Goals & Motivations 2

**Professional Goals:**

1. Keep books current daily (same-day posting)
2. Close month with minimal adjustments
3. Submit PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA on time with complete evidence

**Personal Motivations:**

- Build a reputation for accuracy and reliability
- Progress to Junior/Senior Accountant role
- Reduce after-hours catch-up work

**Success Looks Like:**
No suspense accounts lingering, clean aging, and every submission has its proof attached. Month-end close runs to plan.

### Pain Points & Frustrations 2

**Problems with Current System:**

1. **Problem 1 - Evidence scattered in WhatsApp/email**
   - Frequency: Daily
   - Impact: Posting delays; audit risk
   - Current workaround: Manual downloading to shared folders
   - Quote: “I spend more time hunting PDFs than booking them.”

2. **Problem 2 - Manual reconciliations from multiple payment sources**
   - Frequency: Daily/Weekly
   - Impact: Errors and rework
   - Current workaround: CSV copy/paste and highlight cells
   - Quote: “Give me one place to tick off receipts quickly.”

3. **Problem 3 - Compliance dates tracked in a personal calendar**
   - Frequency: Monthly
   - Impact: Anxiety; occasional slippage
   - Current workaround: Phone reminders and sticky notes
   - Quote: “Remind me inside the tool, not on my wall.”

**Biggest Frustration:**
Posting is blocked because documents or approvals are missing.

**Emotional State:**

- When things go wrong: Conscientious; gets stressed when dependent teams delay docs.

### Needs & Requirements 2

**Must Have (Deal Breakers):**

- [ ] Simple invoice/receipt posting with validations (tax, totals)
- [ ] One evidence vault tied to each transaction/submission
- [ ] Obligation calendar (PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA) with auto-reminders & checklists

**Want to Have (Important):**

- [ ] CSV import with preview & rollback for journals/receipts
- [ ] Aging report with quick “mark as received / promise-to-pay” notes
- [ ] Task list for missing documents or records (assign to ops/sales)

**Nice to Have (Bonus):**

- [ ] One-click compile of submission pack (PDF/ZIP)
- [ ] Light anomaly flags (duplicate invoice number, odd tax)

**Won't Use (Avoid These):**

- ❌ Long multi-page posting forms
- ❌ Black-box auto-posting with no review step
- ❌ Desktop-only experience (needs quick mobile checks)

### How They'll Use Bumara 2

**Frequency:** 6–12 sessions/day (bursty around posting and recon)

**Peak Usage Times:**

- 08:00–10:00 (prior day posting & receipts)
- 15:00–17:30 (recons, reminders, end-of-day tidy)
- Last 5 working days of month (close and submissions)

**Typical Session:**

1. Open “Today” pane: invoices to post, receipts to match, obligations due
2. Post entries (drag/drop evidence or link existing)
3. Reconcile payments; update aging notes
4. 5–12 minutes per block; 1–3 minutes per micro-task

**Device Preference:**

- Desktop: 85%
- Mobile: 15%
- Tablet: 0%

**Top 5 Features They'll Use:**

1. Invoices/Receipts posting with validations
2. Evidence vault storage with access control linked to transactions & obligations
3. CSV import (preview/rollback)
4. Aging & collections notes
5. Compliance checklist & submission record (with acknowledgement storage)

### Quote from Persona 2

> “If I can post with attached proof, reconcile fast, and see filings due in one place, month-end won’t hijack my life.”

### Success Criteria 2

**Mutinta is successful with Bumara when:**

- ✅ Same-day posting rate ≥90%
- ✅ On-time PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA submissions ≥98%
- ✅ Evidence-attached rate =100% for statutory submissions
- ✅ Manual adjustments at close −30% within 2 months
- ✅ Receipt-to-reconciliation cycle time −40%

**Time Saved:** 3–6 hours/week  
**Cost Saved:** Penalties avoided + reduced audit hours
**Stress Reduced:** Fewer after-hours catch-ups; predictable close

## Tertiary Persona: Compliance Consultant (External)

### Quick Summary 3

External specialist hired ad-hoc or on retainer to prepare filings and advise on ZRA/PACRA/NAPSA/NHIMA tasks. Success = fast client onboarding, complete evidence, filings on time, fewer follow-ups and client data management.

### Demographics 3

- **Name:** Kelvin
- **Age:** 48
- **Location:** Kitwe, Zambia
- **Company Type:** Small consultancy (1–4 people)
- **Company Size:** 4
- **Role:** Compliance Consultant
- **Years of Experience:** 10 (practice + regulator familiarity)
- **Tech Savviness:** Advanced with spreadsheets/docs; practical with portals

### Professional Life 3

**Reports To:** Client’s Owner/Finance Manager (per engagement)

**Manages:** Juniors/assistants (0–2)

**Daily Responsibilities:**

- Collect client data/evidence; validate completeness
- Prepare/submit statutory returns; track acknowledgements
- Advise on penalties/objections; maintain calendar of due dates
- Train client staff on basic compliance hygiene

**A Day in their Life:**

Juggles 5–12 clients. Morning: check upcoming deadlines; request missing docs. Midday: compile submissions; attach evidence, follow up with statutory authorities (PACRA, ZRA, NCC). Afternoon: follow up on acknowledgements; send status reports to clients.

### Technical Profile 3

**Current Tools:**

- Email, Excel, cloud drives, regulator portals

**Devices:**

- Laptop primary; phone for reminders

**Computer Skills:** Rate 1-10: 8/10 (spreadsheets, checklists)

**Internet Access:**

- Reliable; mobile hotspot backup

### Goals & Motivations 3

**Professional Goals:**

1. Reduce back-and-forth for missing documents
2. Submit on time with complete evidence
3. Provide simple status reports to clients

**Personal Motivations:**

- Reduce back-and-forth for missing documents
- Submit on time with complete evidence
- Provide simple status reports to clients

### Pain Points & Frustrations 3

**Problems with Current System:**

1. **Problem 1 - Scattered evidence across client emails/WhatsApp → delays**

2. **Problem 2 - Manual reconciliations from multiple payment sources**

3. **Problem 3 - Portal variability (downtime/format changes)**

> Quote: “If clients dropped evidence in the right place and obligations lived in one view, I’d halve my prep time.”

### Needs & Requirements 3

**Must Have (Deal Breakers):**

- [ ] Client-shared vault tied to each obligation
- [ ] Obligation calendar & status per client
- [ ] Submission records with acknowledgements

**Want to Have (Important):**

- [ ] Template checklists per regulator
- [ ] Bulk reminders to client contacts

**Nice to Have (Bonus):**

- [ ] One-click compile of submission pack (PDF/ZIP)
- [ ] Light anomaly flags (duplicate invoice number, odd tax)

**Won't Use (Avoid These):**

- ❌ Systems without per-client segregation
- ❌ Black-box submissions with no proof trail

### How They'll Use Bumara 3

**Frequency:** 1–3 sessions/client/day (burst around due dates)

**Peak Usage Times:** Last 7–10 days of month/quarter

Device Preference: Desktop 85% / Mobile 15%

**Top 5 Features They'll Use:**

1. Obligation calendar & bulk reminder
2. Evidence vault storage per obligation
3. Client switcher & portfolio view

### Quote from Persona 3

> “If I can post with attached proof, reconcile fast, and see filings due in one place, month-end won’t hijack my life.”

### Success Criteria 3

**Mutinta is successful with Bumara when:**

- ✅ Prep time/client −40%
- ✅ On-time submission ≥99%
- ✅ Zero rejected filings due to incomplete docs

**Time Saved:** 5–10 hours/week  
**Cost Saved:** Penalties avoided + reduced audit hours
**Stress Reduced:** Fewer after-hours catch-ups; predictable close
