Every filing needs proof behind it. Bumara keeps that proof attached to the record it belongs to,
so that when a regulator or auditor asks "show me", you open one page rather than searching four
inboxes.

## Document kinds

| Kind | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Something your business produced or received, used as the basis for a filing | Payroll summary, bank statement, sales report |
| **Workpaper** | Working documents supporting a calculation | Reconciliation sheet, computation |
| **Submission** | What was actually sent to the regulator | Completed return form |
| **Receipt** | Proof of payment | Bank confirmation, mobile money receipt |
| **Certificate** | Something issued to you by a regulator | Certificate of incorporation, tax clearance certificate |

Bumara sets the kind automatically when a document satisfies a named requirement. You choose it
when uploading a supporting document yourself.

## Required documents

Each filing and service request lists the documents it requires. The list is not generic — it is
specific to that regulator, that obligation and your entity type.

The documents panel shows, for each requirement:

- The name of what is needed
- Whether it is required or optional
- Whether it has been provided
- The file itself, once uploaded

<Note>
  Uploading a document against a named requirement usually completes the matching "upload
  document" task automatically. You do not upload and then separately tick a box.
</Note>

## Uploading a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filing or service request">
    Go to its Documents panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the requirement you are satisfying">
    Upload against the specific requirement — "Payroll Summary", "Board Resolution". This is what
    links the document to the right task and makes it count towards readiness.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or drag your file">
    PDF is safest for anything official. Photographs of documents are accepted but must be legible
    — a blurry photo of a certificate will be rejected by the regulator, not by Bumara.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the upload to finish">
    Large files take a moment. Do not navigate away until the document appears in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it landed">
    The requirement should now show as satisfied, and the matching task should show as Done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Uploading supporting documents

You can add documents beyond what is required — an explanatory letter, a prior year comparison,
correspondence with the regulator. Use the **Add document** option in the same panel, choose a
kind, and give it a name that someone else would understand in a year's time.

## What makes a good filename

This matters more than it seems. In an audit, a folder of `scan001.pdf` is worthless.

| Instead of | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `scan001.pdf` | `PAYE-summary-2026-03.pdf` |
| `IMG_4471.jpg` | `NAPSA-payment-receipt-2026-03.jpg` |
| `document.pdf` | `Board-resolution-director-change-2026-02-14.pdf` |

## The document vault

Everything you upload, and everything Bumara receives on your behalf, is held in one place.
Open it from Settings, or from a regulator workspace to see only that regulator's documents.

You can filter by:

| Filter | Use it to find |
| --- | --- |
| **Regulator** | Everything filed with or received from ZRA |
| **Kind** | All certificates, or all payment receipts |
| **Linked record** | Everything attached to a specific filing |
| **Date range** | Everything from a particular period |

See [Document vault](/account/document-vault) for organising and exporting.

## Documents Bumara adds for you

You are not the only source. Bumara files these against the right record automatically:

| Document | Added when |
| --- | --- |
| Submission acknowledgement | The Bumara team files your return with the regulator |
| Certificate | A regulator issues one — incorporation, name clearance, tax clearance |
| Payment receipt | A fee payment is confirmed |
| Generated return file | You produce a NAPSA, NHIMA or PAYE return file from payroll |
| Invoice and payslip PDFs | Generated from your invoicing and payroll records |

## Locked documents

Once a filing is submitted, its documents are **locked**. They cannot be replaced or deleted.

This is deliberate. The whole value of the evidence trail is that what was submitted cannot be
changed afterwards. If a locked document is wrong, you do not fix it by swapping the file — you
raise the issue with the Bumara team, and any correction is recorded as a new event with its own
document.

<Warning>
  Deleting a document that satisfies a requirement will reopen its task and take the filing out of
  **Ready for submission**. Check what a document is attached to before removing it.
</Warning>

## Evidence for the health check

Findings from a [compliance health check](/start/compliance-health-check) carry their own
evidence — portal screenshots, tax statements, penalty notices — uploaded by the Bumara team to
support what they found.

If you are disputing a finding, attach your counter-evidence to the dispute. Evidence records are
never edited: adding better evidence means adding a new record, not replacing an old one.

## Practical habits

- **Upload as you go.** Do not save documents for the day before the deadline.
- **Upload the original.** A PDF from your bank beats a screenshot of a PDF from your bank.
- **One document per requirement.** Do not combine three months of statements into one file.
- **Check the vault monthly.** Missing evidence is easiest to chase while people still remember it.

<Note>
  Storage is limited by your plan, with extra storage available as an add-on. See
  [Plan and billing](/account/plan-and-billing).
</Note>
